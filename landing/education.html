How it works

Teach as usual. The recap builds itself.

  1. 1

    Record the lecture

    Press record on a phone or laptop. Teach on your slides or the built-in whiteboard, with pen, highlighter and laser pointer. Every page you use is saved.

  2. 2

    Check it, then send it

    When the lecture ends you get the notes, key points and a five-question quiz to review. Edit anything, then send it to the class as one link. Students don’t need an account.

  3. 3

    See who’s keeping up

    Quiz results per student and per question, how many students opened the notes, and the questions they asked about the lecture.

Questions HoDs ask us

What do faculty need?

A phone or laptop with a browser and a microphone. Nothing to install. A phone on the front desk is enough for most classrooms.

How accurate are the notes?

Notes are built only from the lecture transcript and checked against it, and the teacher reviews every set before it is sent. If something is wrong, the teacher edits it in a few seconds.

Do students need to sign up?

No. Each lecture’s recap is a private link the teacher sends to the class. Students open it on any phone.

What happens to the recordings?

They stay in your department’s PortIQ account. We agree with you how long they are kept, and delete them whenever you ask.

What does it cost after the trial?

Pricing is per department, per semester, and is agreed with you after the trial, before anything is charged.
